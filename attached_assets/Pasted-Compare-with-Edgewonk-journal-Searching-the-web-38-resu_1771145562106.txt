TradifyApp (from @TradifyApp
 and tradifyapp.com) and Edgewonk (edgewonk.com) are both specialized trading journals focused on building discipline and improving execution rather than providing signals or predictions. However, they differ significantly in approach, features, target users, and depth.Core Focus and PhilosophyTradifyApp: Emphasizes strict rule enforcement, pre-trade validation, and psychological discipline through structure. It promotes predefined rules (e.g., position sizing, entry checklists), counters biases like overconfidence or revenge trading, and stresses weekly reviews/journaling to combat memory distortions. It avoids outcome-chasing and prioritizes process over profits. It's built for serious, rule-based traders in niche areas like liquidity concepts.
Edgewonk: Centers on in-depth performance analysis, trading psychology tracking (e.g., Tiltmeter for emotional states, mistake categorization), and uncovering hidden edges via automated insights. It helps traders identify behavioral patterns, refine strategies through data, and includes tools like custom metrics and AI-driven analysis (e.g., Edge Finder for pattern detection in wins/losses).

Which One Fits Better?Choose TradifyApp if you're a disciplined, rule-heavy trader (especially MT5 or prop firm challenges) who needs preventive guardrails to avoid breaking rules in real-time. It's ideal for enforcing structure upfront and tracking prop-specific metrics without fluff.
Choose Edgewonk if you want deeper post-trade analysis, psychology insights, and automated discovery of what truly drives your edge (e.g., via AI pattern detection). It's more comprehensive for long-term behavioral improvement across markets and often ranks highly in 2026 journal comparisons.

Both excel at turning trading into a measurable process, but TradifyApp is more "front-end" discipline-oriented, while Edgewonk is "back-end" insight-heavy. Many serious traders even combine elements (e.g., strict rules + detailed reviews), but starting with one depends on whether prevention or reflection is your bigger gap. If you're MT5/prop-focused, TradifyApp aligns closely with the discipline ethos in its posts. For broader analytics, Edgewonk stands out in reviews.
