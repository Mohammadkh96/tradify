# PROJECT 1 — TRADIFYAPP JOURNAL
## Tradify Ecosystem: Phase 1 — Brand and Website Integration

Domain: https://tradifyapp.com

We have completed our initial Tradify ecosystem architecture documentation.

Our company ecosystem consists of:

1. Tradify Journal — Existing trading journal and performance platform.
2. Tradify Signals — Separate algorithmic signals product at https://signals.tradifyapp.com.
3. Tradigo — Future coaching marketplace.

The goal is to establish one professional Tradify brand while keeping each product independently deployable.

## STEP 1 — Review the Existing Architecture

Read:

- docs/TRADIFY_ECOSYSTEM.md
- docs/INTEGRATION_CONTRACTS.md

Audit the current marketing website, navigation, branding, landing page, pricing sections, registration entry points, and existing customer dashboard.

Preserve all Journal functionality.

Do not assume the Signals project uses the same authentication, subscriptions, or database.

Provide the contents of INTEGRATION_CONTRACTS.md for external architecture review, excluding any sensitive credentials.

## STEP 2 — Design the Parent Tradify Brand

Prepare a unified branding proposal for the ecosystem.

The parent brand will be Tradify.

The products will be:

- Tradify Journal
- Tradify Signals
- Future Tradigo

Create a consistent design system covering:

- Brand identity.
- Primary and secondary colors.
- Typography.
- Buttons.
- Navigation.
- Product cards.
- Headers and footers.
- Mobile-responsive behavior.
- Shared components where appropriate.

Preserve the recognizable existing Tradify Journal identity where possible.

Do not perform a wholesale visual redesign without approval.

Document proposed shared design tokens so the independent Signals project can reproduce them.

## STEP 3 — Introduce Both Products on the Website

Prepare enhancements to the public TradifyApp website.

### Homepage

Introduce the wider Tradify ecosystem without removing the existing Journal marketing content.

Explain that Tradify provides tools for:

- Trading performance analysis.
- MT5 trade tracking.
- Prop-firm challenge monitoring.
- Risk management.
- Algorithmic market signals.
- Trading notifications.

Add a professional product showcase:

**Tradify Journal**
Track performance, synchronize trading accounts, monitor risk and analyze trading results.

**Tradify Signals**
Follow algorithmic trading strategies, monitor entries and targets, and access trading signal updates.

Each product should have a clearly identified call to action.

Journal links should retain their existing destination.

Signals links should point to https://signals.tradifyapp.com once the destination is confirmed working.

### Navigation

Prepare a Products menu containing:

- Tradify Journal
- Tradify Signals

Reserve future extensibility for Tradigo without displaying it as an available product.

Do not change existing login routes, user dashboard routes, or authenticated navigation unexpectedly.

### Footer

Add clear ecosystem links, product descriptions, and relevant legal navigation.

## STEP 4 — Prepare Combined Pricing

Design a pricing presentation supporting:

- Journal Only
- Signals Only
- Tradify Complete — Journal + Signals

IMPORTANT:

Do not activate combined billing.

Do not invent pricing.

Do not create discounts, checkout sessions, or subscription entitlements.

Do not modify existing Stripe or PayPal integrations.

First identify current Journal subscription tiers and document how a future bundle might work.

Mark combined pricing as a proposal until both products have an approved billing contract.

## STEP 5 — Preserve Existing Journal Operations

Do not modify:

- Original Neon production database.
- MT5 synchronization.
- Customer records.
- Trading history.
- Prop-firm challenges.
- Risk calculations.
- Existing paid subscriptions.
- Stripe or PayPal webhooks.
- Background workers.
- Domain DNS configuration.

Do not introduce AI inference functionality.

The Journal production restoration remains a separate priority.

## STEP 6 — Implementation Approach

First provide:

1. Current website design audit.
2. Proposed unified branding.
3. Existing design components that can be reused.
4. Homepage and navigation changes.
5. Pricing presentation proposal.
6. Potential compatibility risks.

Then implement approved, non-destructive branding and navigation changes in the development environment.

Do not publish to production.

Run applicable typechecks, builds and visual tests.

Use temporary-safe navigation behavior if Signals production is not ready.

## FINAL DELIVERABLES

Provide:

- Development preview URL.
- Updated homepage and navigation preview.
- Unified design tokens.
- Proposed pricing presentation.
- List of modified files.
- Test results.
- Integration contract document for comparison.
- Cross-project handoff instructions for Tradify Signals.

Do not modify the Signals repository or database.

Do not publish, create paid deployments, activate workers or modify production without explicit approval.

The success criterion is that TradifyApp presents a coherent multi-product brand while preserving the original Journal customer experience.
