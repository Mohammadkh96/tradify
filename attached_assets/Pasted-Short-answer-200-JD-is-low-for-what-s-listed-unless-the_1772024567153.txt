Short answer: 200 JD is low for what’s listed — unless the scope is very limited.

Let’s break it down professionally.

The offer includes:

Project growth plan + client acquisition system

Content writing

Ad campaign management

These are three separate service categories:

Strategy/consulting

Content production

Performance marketing (ads management)

In most markets (including Jordan and GCC), pricing typically looks like this:

Growth strategy alone: 150–500 JD (one-time, depending on depth)

Content writing: 100–300 JD/month (depending on volume)

Ads management: 150–400 JD/month (excluding ad spend)

So bundled together, fair market value would normally sit around:
➡ 350–800 JD depending on depth and deliverables.

Now the real question:

When is 200 JD acceptable?

If this is a starter freelancer trying to build a portfolio

If the growth plan is basic (not detailed funnel architecture)

If content is limited (e.g., 4–8 posts only)

If ad management is simple setup + light optimization

If it’s a short-term trial (1 month)

When is 200 JD underpriced?

If they’re promising a full acquisition system

If they’re managing multiple campaigns

If they’re producing significant content volume

If they’re expected to handle reporting, optimization, and scaling

Because realistically, ads management alone can consume 15–25 hours per month if done properly.

Strategic Advice

If you’re the buyer:
Ask for clear scope definition:

How many posts?

How many campaigns?

What platforms?

How many revisions?

Is reporting included?

Is this monthly or one-time?

If you’re the seller:
At 200 JD, you’re either:

Undervaluing your work

Or offering a very light version of these services

You could restructure like:

200 JD: Strategy + setup only

350 JD: Full monthly management

Professional Verdict

200 JD is:

Good price for the client

Risky price for the provider (unless scope is very controlled)

Are you the one paying or the one offering the service?
