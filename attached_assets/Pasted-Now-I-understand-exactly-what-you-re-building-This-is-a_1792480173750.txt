Now I understand exactly what you're building. This is a much stronger concept than simply connecting Journal and Signals or selling trading signals.

You're proposing that Tradify Signals has two signal sources, powered by the same strategy engine:
1. Tradify Signals: Strategies run on market data supplied by your own Tradify MT5 Bridge.
2. My Signals: The same strategies run using market data supplied by the trader's own MT5 Bridge, connected through Tradify Journal.
And here's the key advantage: when traders use their own MT5 connection, Tradify Journal can analyze their actual trades alongside the signals generated for their broker.

That's the ecosystem I would build.

2. What the customer actually experiences
Imagine someone registering with Tradify.
Step A — They register with Tradify

They can immediately open Signals and view opportunities calculated from your own market-data feed.

They do not need an MT5 account to start exploring.

Step B — They connect their MT5 through Journal

Journal discovers their broker, trading account, supported symbols and historical executions. If the bridge supports market-data delivery, it can also make that broker's prices and candles available to Signals with the user's permission.

Step C — Signals unlocks My Signals

The same Tradify strategies now evaluate the customer's selected broker feed.

They can switch between Tradify's signals and their own broker-specific signals.

Step D — They execute trades

The customer continues trading in MT5. Journal automatically imports real executions, associates them with relevant signals where the match is supported, and measures actual performance.

Step E — Tradify helps them improve

They can compare signal performance against actual trading results, identifying entry delays, spread differences, risk sizing and execution mistakes.

4. An example using Gold (XAUUSD)
Suppose the same Tradify algorithm runs on two different market feeds.
	Tradify Signals	My MT5 Signals
Source	Tradify reference broker	Customer's broker
Instrument	XAUUSD	XAUUSD
Strategy	Same algorithm	Same algorithm
Chart data	Tradify bridge	Customer MT5 bridge
Entry calculation	Based on Tradify prices	Based on broker prices
SL / TP	Reference-feed levels	Broker-feed levels
Actual execution	Not assumed	Journal tracks it
Performance	Strategy model	Strategy result + real customer executions
The algorithm can be identical while its outputs differ slightly because brokers have different price feeds, spreads, trading conditions, and candle construction.

For example, a Gold breakout could trigger on the reference feed but not yet trigger on the trader's broker feed. That difference is precisely why a personal-feed signal mode could be valuable.

One important caveat: connecting MT5 for trade-history synchronization does not automatically mean the bridge delivers usable live ticks and historical candles. We need to verify that capability in your existing Tradify Bridge before promising broker-specific signals.
5. What should remain in Journal versus Signals?
Tradify Journal owns	Tradify Signals owns
MT5 account onboarding and connections	Signal source selection
MT5 credentials and connection health	Strategy engine and calculation rules
Actual trading accounts and executions	Global and personal signal generation
Trade history and realized P&L	Chart-based signal conditions
Risk and prop-firm metrics	Entry, SL, TP, signal lifecycle
Trade-to-signal attribution	Signal alerts and monitoring
Execution performance analysis	Comparative signal statistics
I would not duplicate the complete Journal analytics system inside Signals.

Signals should be the place for what the market and strategies are suggesting. Journal should be the place for what the customer actually did and achieved.
6. Technical decisions I strongly recommend
The biggest benefit will come from sharing the strategy engine, not from creating separate algorithms for each customer.
- One versioned strategy engine: The same strategy definition evaluates Tradify's feed or an individual broker feed. Every resulting signal records its source, strategy version, timeframe and evaluation timestamp.
- One MT5 Bridge ownership layer: Journal owns customer connections. Signals receives only approved market-data streams and account context; it does not need broker passwords.
- Separate results per source: A customer-specific signal must never overwrite or be confused with a global Tradify signal.
- Journal-aware personal signals: Eventually, account risk, drawdown, current positions and prop-firm limits can inform risk warnings or suppress unsuitable setups, while clearly distinguishing this from the underlying market signal.
- Cost-controlled computation: Evaluate reference-market strategies once where possible; create per-account broker evaluations only for connected, subscribed accounts and necessary instruments/timeframes. Avoid one constant database-polling worker for every trader.
I would keep both source modes signal-only, with no automatic order execution in V1. The customer's MT5 connection provides data and execution history, not authorization for Tradify to place trades.
7. The real competitive advantage
This gives you three layers of value.
Discover

Tradify provides strategies and trading opportunities without requiring customers to connect a broker.



Personalize

The trader selects their own MT5 market feed and runs the same algorithms against their broker's actual market data.



Improve

Journal measures how the trader actually executed, how their performance compares with the signal, and where improvements may be possible.



This is considerably more differentiated than selling the same signals to everyone.

The customer gets both market-wide strategy insights and a personalized view based on their broker environment, with Journal automatically measuring their real trading results.

The next technical decision is crucial: We need to audit the existing Tradify MT5 Bridge and determine whether it currently sends live prices and OHLC candles, or only positions, orders, deals and account history.

If it supports both, much of your concept may be achievable by extending the current integration. If it only synchronizes trading activity, we'll need to add a properly isolated market-data pipeline before personal signals can work as described.

I would make that the next development milestone—before building more strategy engines or duplicated charts.
