PROJECT: ORIGINAL TRADIFYAPP JOURNAL

The Tradify Signals Agent has reviewed our proposed account-linking V1 protocol.

The overall architecture is acceptable, but Signals identified security and compatibility requirements that must be resolved before either project enables linking.

Please revise docs/TRADIFY_ACCOUNT_LINKING_V1.md into a complete, implementation-ready V1 contract.

Required amendments:

1. SIGNALS IDENTITY
Use a stable, server-generated opaque Signals linking subject. Never trust a client-supplied registration userId or match accounts by email.

2. EXPLICIT CONSENT
Require independently authenticated Journal and Signals sessions, with separate, versioned consent records in both products.

3. SECURE LINKING FLOW
Define the complete user journey, including how the Journal authorization code reaches Signals without placing sensitive tokens in URLs, logs, analytics or persistent browser storage.

The customer must know exactly which two accounts are being linked.

4. SIGNED ASSERTIONS
Specify:
- RS256 signed assertions.
- Environment-specific issuer and audience.
- Server-side signing and verification.
- Pinned signing key IDs.
- Maximum assertion lifetime of 120 seconds.
- Exact clock-skew rules.
- Key rotation and emergency revocation.

5. REPLAY AND SUBSTITUTION PREVENTION
Specify atomic consumption of the single-use authorization code, complete claim comparison for idempotent retries, uniqueness constraints, CSRF protection and rejection of account substitution.

6. ACCOUNT LIFECYCLE
Define:
- Unlink from either product.
- Account deletion.
- Account suspension.
- Lost account access and recovery.
- Link retention and revoked audit records.
- Failed or interrupted linking.
- Reconciliation if one service becomes unavailable.

Unlinking must not delete the other account, change its subscriptions or transfer permissions.

7. SECURITY BOUNDARIES
Keep both databases, passwords, customer sessions, Admin permissions and product entitlements independent.

8. DEVELOPMENT INTEGRATION
Provide exact versioned request/response contracts, example sanitized payloads, endpoint ownership, failure responses, required schema changes, and contract tests.

Prepare a development-only implementation plan that uses separate isolated databases and synthetic accounts.

IMPORTANT:

Do not enable the existing Journal linking feature.

Do not apply migrations to production.

Do not activate shared SSO or subscription synchronization.

Do not deploy, change DNS or modify Signals.

Do not weaken existing security requirements to simplify the integration.

- Revised complete account-linking V1 contract.
- Clear responsibilities for Journal and Signals.
- Exact implementation and testing handoff.
- Updated contract tests where possible.
- Commit hash.
- Downloadable Markdown handoff for the Signals Agent.

Once completed, stop and wait for Signals' acceptance before activating or implementing cross-project functionality.
