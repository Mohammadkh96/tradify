PHASE 1 Critical Technical Fixes Week 1–2 ▾
01
Fix Server-Side Rendering (SSR) — MOST URGENT
Your site renders only in JavaScript. Google sees an empty page. You need SSR or Static Site Generation (SSG). If using React: migrate to Next.js. If on WordPress/Webflow, this may already be fixed. Every day this isn't fixed = zero organic growth from SEO.
SEOTECH
Impact10x
02
Add Unique Meta Titles & Descriptions Per Page
Right now every page has the same generic meta. Each page needs a unique, keyword-rich title. Example — Pricing page: "TradifyApp Pricing | Free & Pro Plans for Prop Traders". Features page: "MT5 Auto-Sync & Live Drawdown Tracking | TradifyApp".
SEO
ImpactHigh
03
Submit XML Sitemap to Google Search Console
Verify ownership of tradifyapp.com in Google Search Console (if not done), submit your sitemap, and request indexing of all key pages: /, /features, /pricing, /signup, /resources. Monitor coverage errors weekly.
SEOTECH
ImpactHigh
04
Add OG Tags for Social Sharing
When anyone shares your link on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or Discord, it should show a branded image card. Add og:title, og:description, og:image (1200×630px branded graphic), and twitter:card to your HTML head. Right now it likely shows a blank or broken preview.
SEOUX
ImpactMed
05
Fix Page Load Speed
Run your site through pagespeed.web.dev. Common JS-heavy site issues: uncompressed images, no lazy loading, blocking scripts. Target: LCP under 2.5s, FID under 100ms. Google uses Core Web Vitals as a ranking signal and users bounce after 3 seconds.
TECHSEO
ImpactMed
PHASE 2 Homepage Conversion Overhaul Week 2–4 ▾
06
Rewrite the Hero Section — Lead with the Outcome, Not the Feature
Your current headline talks about what the app does. Change it to what the trader gets. Example: "Stop Failing Prop Challenges. Start Trading with Rules That Actually Stick." — Then subtitle: "TradifyApp enforces your trading rules automatically. MT5 auto-sync. Live drawdown alerts. Revenge trading prevention." This is the highest-leverage copywriting change you can make.
CONVUX
ImpactHigh+
07
Add a Social Proof Bar Below the Hero
After your headline, before anything else, show: "Trusted by 2,400+ prop traders" (or your real number) + 3–5 star reviews with names/photos + logos of prop firms your users trade with (FTMO, MyFundedFX, Apex, etc.). If you don't have enough reviews yet — get 10 this week by emailing your free users. Social proof is the #1 trust signal on a SaaS landing page.
CONV
ImpactHigh+
08
Add a Live Demo Video or Animated GIF in the Hero
The fastest way to explain a trading tool is to show it working. Add a 60–90 second screen recording of: (1) a rule being set, (2) a trade being blocked, (3) drawdown being tracked. Place this video next to or below your headline. Loom or Tella.tv are free recording tools. This single addition typically lifts trial signups by 25–40%.
CONVUX
ImpactHigh+
09
Build a "Pain-Driven" Feature Section
Don't just list features — pair each feature with the pain it removes. Structure: "The Problem → The Solution". Example: "You break your daily loss rule under pressure → TradifyApp locks your account automatically when the limit is hit." Do this for 4–5 core features. Pain-first framing converts 2–3x better than feature-first.
CONVUX
ImpactHigh
10
Add a "How It Works" Section (3 Steps, Max)
Visitors need to visualize using the product in 10 seconds. Three steps only: (1) Connect your MT5 → (2) Set your trading rules → (3) Trade with automatic enforcement. Use icons + a short sentence each. Put this between the features section and pricing. Eliminates the #1 objection: "I don't understand how it works."
CONVUX
ImpactHigh
11
Redesign the Pricing Page with an Anchor Strategy
Use 3 tiers with the middle one "recommended": Free → Pro (~$19/mo) → Power (~$39/mo). Make Power feel like a deal vs. Pro. Add a feature comparison table. Most importantly — add the annual vs. monthly toggle (annual pricing creates commitment). And show a "Most Popular" badge on the middle tier. This framing alone can double Pro conversions.

ImpactHigh

PHASE 4 Conversion & Retention Mechanics Month 2–4 ▾
16
Build an Onboarding Email Sequence (7-Day)
The biggest churn driver for SaaS is users who sign up and never activate. Build a 7-email sequence: Day 0 (Welcome + connect MT5 tutorial), Day 1 (Set your first rule), Day 3 (See your first discipline report), Day 5 (Case study: trader who passed FTMO using TradifyApp), Day 7 (Upgrade prompt). Use Mailchimp or ConvertKit. Activation email sequences reduce 30-day churn by 40–60%.
