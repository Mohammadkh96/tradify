Built for focus

Useful context.
Clear boundaries.

Each part of the workspace has a job: help you prepare, monitor, and learn without suggesting certainty.

A.

Configured markets, on your terms

Explore the available market catalog and save a personal watchlist within the limits of your membership.

B.

Curated qualified opportunities

Review opportunities selected for qualification, with relevant context surfaced when it is available and validated.

C.

Setup context and levels that earn their place

When the underlying data passes validation, view trusted setup context and levels such as entry, stop, and target. If it does not, context may be limited or unavailable.

D.

Watchlists and price alerts

Save the markets you follow and set alerts around levels you care about, subject to your membership access.

E.

Risk planning alongside the idea

Use account size and risk preferences as planning inputs. Calculations are illustrative and do not control execution or limit losses.

Performance, in context

Your record.
Shown plainly.

Performance context belongs to your account. It is calculated from positions you have recorded and closed, so it reflects your own journal rather than a claimed service-wide result.

Use it to review process and outcomes—not to infer what a future trade will do.

How it is measured

Account-local. Record-based.

In-app performance context uses your account’s recorded closed trades and their saved trade details.

R-based resultsOutcomes are expressed in R relative to recorded risk.
Recent outcomesReview the recent closed-position window available in the app.
Closed positionsOpen or unrecorded positions do not establish a completed result.
Your account onlyThis is personal performance context, not a pooled track record.

These results are not an independently audited or guaranteed strategy track record. Records may be incomplete or entered incorrectly. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
