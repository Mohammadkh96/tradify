Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information: email address, display name, password credentials in protected form, account status, and session information when you create or use an account.
  • Trader and product information: watchlist symbols, price alerts, trade journal records, notification read state, performance records, risk preferences, selected strategy, and onboarding choices.
  • Subscription information: membership tier, subscription status, expiration or renewal state, store transaction identifiers, and billing recovery information needed to confirm paid access. Payments are processed through Apple, Google, and the billing services used to verify store purchases; Tradify Signals does not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Device and technical information: information needed to keep a session working, remember selected local preferences, deliver the app, diagnose failures, and maintain security. Some preferences and offline records are stored on your device through the app’s local storage.
  • Market information: market quotes, instrument selections, and signal context used to provide the product. Market-provider and broker diagnostics are used for service operations and are not presented as customer account data.

How we use information

  • Provide market views, opportunity context, alerts, trade planning, performance views, account access, and settings.
  • Sync account-owned records across supported sessions and devices when you sign in.
  • Confirm purchases, restore access, reconcile subscription state, and prevent unauthorized billing changes.
  • Secure the service, prevent abuse, investigate failures, and maintain reliable operations.
  • Respond to support or account requests and communicate service or policy changes when necessary.
